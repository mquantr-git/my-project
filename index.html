Break BFCM sales record with BOGO, buy x get y, free gift, bundles, discounts, and checkout upsell

Over 12 years, 95K+ brands have trusted BOGOS to run offers seamlessly at scale, drive more orders, and elevate shopping experience. An easy-to-use app for creating BOGO, free gifts with purchase, bundles, discounts, and upsells. All widgets are fully customizable and built to convert. BOGOS supports POS, Headless API, auto-sync inventory, targeting, translation, and subscription. Prompt human chat, technical support are always available, backed by helpful video tutorials & AI assistant.

  • Auto-add to cart or show a free gift popup for buy one get one, BXGY, and GWP
  • Create product bundles, bundle builder (build a box), and mix and match bundles
  • Set up volume discounts, tiered discounts, cart discount, discount on cheapest
  • Checkout upsell using discount code, frequently bought together & thank you page
  • Promote offers with progress bar, announcement bar, today offer, and offer page
